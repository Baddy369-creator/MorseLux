How the trainer works

  1. Listen to the two first characters, K and M, then press Start.
  2. Answer each sound by tapping the letter or pressing the key. After a wrong answer you hear the right character and then yours, so you can hear the difference.
  3. Reach 90 % in a round of 20 and a new character is unlocked. Characters you mix up come back more often.
  4. From level 4: practice groups of three characters, like real copying — optionally with longer pauses.

Tips from experienced operators

  • Don't count. If you can no longer count the dots, the speed is right — trust the sound.
  • Short and often. Two rounds a day beat an hour on Sunday.
  • Guess. A quick guess trains your ear better than a long pause.
  • Look up the patterns later. The Morse code alphabet is useful as a reference, and the learning guide explains the whole path.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Koch method?

A way of learning Morse developed by the psychologist Ludwig Koch: you listen at full character speed from the start, begin with just two characters and add one more each time you reach 90 % accuracy. Because you never count dots and dashes, you learn to recognise each character as a sound — which is how experienced operators read Morse.

Why 20 WPM and not slower?

At slow speeds every character sounds like a sequence of dots and dashes you have to count. At 20 WPM it becomes one sound pattern, like a word. That is harder for the first few days but much faster in the long run. Choose 15 WPM if 20 feels impossible.

How long does it take to learn Morse code?

It differs a lot from person to person. With 10–15 minutes a day, many people know all 40 characters by ear after one to three months. Short daily sessions work much better than one long session per week.

Which order are the characters in?

The usual Koch order: K M R S U A P T L O W I . N J E F 0 Y , V G 5 / Q 9 Z H 3 8 B ? 4 2 7 C 1 D 6 X. It mixes short and long characters early so that you do not rely on length.

Why do I only get half a point?

If you play a character again before answering, a correct answer counts as half. That way you cannot reach 90 % by counting dots — the trainer rewards recognising the sound at once.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device (no account needed). If you clear your browser data or use another device, you start again.
