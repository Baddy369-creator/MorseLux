One word a day per level, the same for everyone. Pick easy, medium or hard, listen or read the dots and dashes – and share your result without spoiling it.

Morse puzzle

How to play

  1. Listen or read. Tap “Listen” or decode the dots and dashes.
  2. Guess. Type a word with the right number of letters.
  3. Use the colours. Green: right letter, right place. Yellow: in the word, other place.
  4. Share. Post your grid and challenge your friends – the answer stays secret.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the levels?

Easy: short words, slow sound, 6 tries and a hint. Medium: 5–6 letters with dots and dashes shown, 4 tries. Hard: long words, faster sound and only by ear – the dots and dashes appear when you are done.

How does the Morse puzzle work?

Every day there is one new word in Morse code. Listen to it or read the signs, type your guess and you get colours: green means the right letter in the right place, yellow means the letter is in the word somewhere else. You have four tries.

When is there a new puzzle?

Every day at midnight, your local time. Each level has its own word and its own streak.

How is the word of the day chosen?

Each level has a fixed list of words in a shuffled order. Day 1 was 1 November 2026, and on day n everyone gets word number n of that list – so the puzzle is the same for everybody, wherever they are.

Can I share my result?

Yes. “Share result” copies a grid of coloured squares without the answer, so you can post it without spoiling the puzzle.

I don't know Morse code yet – can I still play?

Yes. Keep the Morse alphabet open in another tab, or learn the letters in the free Morse trainer.
