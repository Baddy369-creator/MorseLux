How to send “No” in Morse code

“No” has 1 dots and 4 dashes. At 15 words per minute it takes about 1.5 seconds. Tap short for a dot and three times as long for a dash, with a short pause between the letters and a longer one between words. Play it as sound or light in the Morse code translator, or type it in WhatsApp with the Morse keyboard for iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write “No” in Morse code?

“No” in Morse code is –· ––– (written with a keyboard: -. ---). Letters are separated by a space and words by a slash.

How many dots and dashes are in “No”?

1 dots and 4 dashes. Sent at 15 words per minute it lasts about 1.5 seconds.
